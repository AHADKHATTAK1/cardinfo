FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about VaultID digital passes.

How do members add their card to Apple Wallet & Google Wallet?

Members simply open their VaultID card on any browser, tap "Add to Wallet", and select Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. iPhone users receive a signed .pkpass file and Android users get a direct Google Pay link that installs in one tap.

Do members need active internet connection for NFC scanning?

No! Once saved to Apple Wallet, Google Wallet, or installed as a PWA, identity credentials and encoded NFC/QR tokens work 100% offline without requiring cellular data or Wi-Fi.

How does the 10% platform commission work for organizations?

If your organization charges members a subscription or membership fee, VaultID automatically processes transactions and retains a 10% platform commission while remitting 90% net earnings directly to your merchant account.

Can we customize cards with our university/company logo and brand colors?

Yes! VaultID features a live drag-and-drop Card Designer supporting custom high-res logo uploads, 12 curated HSL color themes, custom field definitions, and custom organization icons.
